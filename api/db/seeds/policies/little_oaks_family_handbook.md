# Little Oaks Early Learning Family Handbook

## Tuition due dates
Tuition is billed monthly and is due on the 1st of each month. Invoices are posted in the Little Oaks app five days before the due date. Tuition covers the full month regardless of absences, closures for weather, or holidays.

## Late payments and late fees
Payments received after the 5th of the month are late. A late fee of $35 is added to any balance still open on the 10th of the month. We waive one late fee per family per school year on request, no questions asked. Balances more than 30 days past due may lead to a pause in care until a payment plan is in place.

## Payment plans
We would rather work something out than lose a family. Any family can ask the director for a payment plan. Plans usually split an open balance across two to four payments alongside regular tuition. Families on a plan that stays current do not pay late fees on the planned balance. Financial conversations stay between the family and the director.

## Payment methods
We accept ACH bank transfer and debit or credit card through the Little Oaks app, and checks dropped at the front desk. Card payments carry a 2.9% processing fee; bank transfers are free.

## Waitlist
Families can join the waitlist at any time with a requested start date. Siblings of enrolled children receive priority, then families by date joined. We contact waitlisted families when a seat in the right age group opens near their requested start date. Families may update their start date or leave the waitlist at any time.

## Enrollment offers
When we offer a seat, we hold it for five business days while the family decides. To accept, families complete the enrollment packet and pay a one-time $150 registration fee, which is applied to the first month's tuition. If we don't hear back within five business days, we offer the seat to the next family and keep yours on the waitlist.

## Classrooms and ratios
Little Oaks follows Minnesota licensing ratios: one teacher for every four infants, seven toddlers, or ten preschoolers. Directors do not count toward ratio. When a teacher is out, we call in a floater or substitute before the day begins.

## Illness
Children with a fever of 100.4 F or higher, vomiting, or diarrhea stay home until they have been symptom free for 24 hours without medication. We call families to pick up a child who becomes ill during the day within one hour.

## Late pickup
We close at 6:00 PM. A late pickup fee of $1 per minute applies after 6:05 PM. After three late pickups in a month, the director will set up a conversation about the family's schedule.

## Communication and languages
We send messages through the Little Oaks app. Interpretation is available in Spanish, Somali, and Hmong at no cost; ask the front desk and we will arrange it for conversations and translated notices.

## Withdrawal
Families give 30 days' written notice before withdrawing. Tuition is due through the end of the notice period.
